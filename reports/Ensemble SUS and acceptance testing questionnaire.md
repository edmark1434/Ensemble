# Ensemble Alpha Testing Questionnaire: System Usability Scale (SUS) + User Acceptance Testing (UAT)

**Who answers:** alpha testers after they finish the scripted tasks for each module.
**Time:** about 20–25 minutes if every module is tested. Testers pick **N/A** for anything they did not try.
**Sample size:** at least **15 testers**; 30 or more is better. SUS gives stable means from about 12 respondents up.

---

## Rating scales (every scored question uses 1–5)

### Scale A: Agreement (used in Parts 1, 2 and 3)

| Value | Label |
|---|---|
| 5 | Strongly Agree |
| 4 | Agree |
| 3 | Neutral |
| 2 | Disagree |
| 1 | Strongly Disagree |

### Scale B: Task acceptance (used in Part 4, one rating per test task)

| Value | Label | Meaning for the tester |
|---|---|---|
| 5 | Excellent | Worked smoothly, with no problems |
| 4 | Good | Worked as expected, with small hiccups |
| 3 | Fair | Worked, but with noticeable problems or confusion |
| 2 | Poor | Barely worked; needed help or many retries |
| 1 | Failed | Could not finish the task |
| N/A | Did not try | Leave out of all calculations |

> **Google Forms setup:** for each section, use a **Multiple-choice grid**. Rows are the questions and columns are 1–5 (plus N/A in Part 4). Turn on "Require a response in each row" so there are no blanks.

## How to compute the statistics

### 1. Weighted mean per item (Parts 2, 3 and 4)

```
WM = Σ (f × x) / N
f = number of testers who chose value x (1–5)
N = number of testers who answered (exclude N/A)
```

Also report the **standard deviation** and the **n** for each item, plus **% rating 4–5** (top-2-box) and **% rating 1–2** (bottom-2-box).

### 2. Verbal interpretation (5-point range: (5−1)/5 = 0.80 per band)

| Mean range | Scale A (agreement) | Scale B (task acceptance) | Acceptance decision |
|---|---|---|---|
| 4.21 – 5.00 | Strongly Agree | Excellent | Highly Acceptable |
| 3.41 – 4.20 | Agree | Good | Acceptable |
| 2.61 – 3.40 | Neutral | Fair | Moderately Acceptable (needs fixes) |
| 1.81 – 2.60 | Disagree | Poor | Slightly Acceptable |
| 1.00 – 1.80 | Strongly Disagree | Failed | Not Acceptable |

### 3. Composite scores

| Score | How to compute |
|---|---|
| Each ISO characteristic (FS, PE, CO, US, RE, SE, PO) | Mean of its items |
| Overall ISO 25010 quality | Mean of the 7 characteristic means |
| PU, PEU, BI (TAM) | Mean of each construct's items |
| Each module acceptance score (VE, JG, CF, AM, TM, CW, GP) | Mean of its task items (N/A excluded) |
| Overall module acceptance | Mean of the 7 module means |

### 4. SUS score (Part 1 only, with its own formula; do not average SUS answers as 1–5)

For each tester:

```
Odd items  (1,3,5,7,9):  contribution = answer − 1
Even items (2,4,6,8,10): contribution = 5 − answer
SUS score = (sum of all 10 contributions) × 2.5      → 0 to 100
```

Then report the **mean SUS score** across testers (± standard deviation / confidence interval).

**Grade (Sauro–Lewis curved grading scale)**

| SUS score | Grade | SUS score | Grade |
|---|---|---|---|
| 84.1 – 100 | A+ | 71.1 – 72.5 | C+ |
| 80.8 – 84.0 | A | 65.0 – 71.0 | C |
| 78.9 – 80.7 | A− | 62.7 – 64.9 | C− |
| 77.2 – 78.8 | B+ | 51.7 – 62.6 | D |
| 74.1 – 77.1 | B | 0 – 51.6 | F |
| 72.6 – 74.0 | B− | | |

**Acceptability range (Bangor, Kortum & Miller)**

| SUS score | Acceptability |
|---|---|
| 70 – 100 | Acceptable |
| 50 – 69.9 | Marginal |
| 0 – 49.9 | Not acceptable |

**Benchmark:** the industry average SUS score is **68**. A score of **68 or above** means usability is above average, and **≥ 70** is the usual "acceptable" threshold.

### 5. Acceptance criteria (set these before testing starts)

A module **passes UAT** when all of these hold:
1. Module acceptance mean is **≥ 3.41** (Good / Acceptable).
2. No single task in the module has **more than 20%** of testers rating it 1–2.
3. No task has a mean **below 2.61**. Any task below that is a blocker and goes on the fix list.

The **whole system is accepted** when:
- Mean SUS is **≥ 68**,
- Overall ISO 25010 mean and TAM BI mean are each **≥ 3.41**, and
- Every module passes UAT (or the failing ones have a documented fix plan).

### 6. Reliability check (optional, for a thesis or paper)

Compute **Cronbach's alpha** for each multi-item group (SUS, each ISO characteristic, PU, PEU, BI, and each module). **α ≥ 0.70** means the items measure the construct consistently. Report it next to each composite score.
