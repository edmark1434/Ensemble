# Ensemble Platform History and Background

Route: `/landing/AboutUs#history`

## Origins and name

Ensemble was created by the **RavenLabs Development** engineering team. What began as a last-minute addition to a project pitch quickly stood out to reviewers for combining real-time browser video collaboration with a full creative marketplace.

The name **Ensemble** reflects working together as a unified team ("Collab Ensemble"), bringing video editing, job hiring, packaged services, and asset trading together under one platform.
