# Reports and Disputes

Routes: `/contracts/dispute-form`, `/contracts/disputes`, `/contracts`, `/support/ticket`

Ensemble provides separate workflows for reporting rule violations and resolving contract or transaction disputes.

## Reporting content or users

If you encounter spam, harassment, scams, stolen assets, fake listings, or inappropriate behavior, you can report the item directly from the platform using the in-app report button on a profile, forum post, job, gig, or asset listing, or by opening a report ticket on `/support/ticket`.

When you submit a report, choose the reason and provide clear details. Filing a report alerts Ensemble's review team so they can inspect the reported content and account history. A report by itself does not automatically penalize an account or prove a violation.

## Filing a contract or transaction dispute

Disputes apply to active or recently closed **contracts and transactions** when the client and freelancer cannot resolve a delivery, quality, or cancellation disagreement on their own. You can file a dispute from a contract's detail page or on `/contracts/dispute-form`.

When a contract dispute is opened:

- **Escrow freeze:** Any remaining credits held in escrow for that contract are frozen immediately so neither party can release, refund, or cancel them until the dispute is resolved.
- **Track your cases:** You can view and follow all of your open and past disputes on `/contracts/disputes`.

## Hidden vs. visible disputes

Disputes may start as private (visible only to the person who filed and Ensemble reviewers) or become visible to both parties:

- **Hidden dispute:** While hidden, only the filer can view the case and send additional information or attachments to support. The other party cannot see or reply to it yet.
- **Visible dispute:** Once visible, both parties can view the dispute thread, post messages and evidence, and communicate with support until a decision is reached.

You can also view a public dispute discussion thread at its shared dispute link when applicable.

## Dispute outcomes and appeals

Ensemble reviews submitted evidence, contract milestones, and delivery history before issuing a ruling (such as releasing escrow to the freelancer, refunding escrow to the client, splitting funds, or dismissing the claim). If a party is found responsible for serious misconduct in a dispute conviction, their account may be restricted or permanently banned.

Opening a legitimate dispute does not deduct Platform Merit. However, filing false or abusive disputes can result in a warning for abuse of the dispute process. If you believe a closed dispute decision was made in error, contact support via `/support/ticket` or `/account/appeal` (if restricted) to request an appeal.
