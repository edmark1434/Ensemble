# Platform Merit

Routes: `/profile`, `/settings`

Platform Merit is Ensemble's account reliability score. It measures rule compliance and account trustworthiness on a scale from 0 to 100, separate from your client or freelancer star ratings.

## How Platform Merit works

Every account starts with a Platform Merit score of 100. Active, unexpired warnings issued on the account temporarily deduct merit based on the severity of the warning. Once a warning expires or is rescinded, its merit deduction ends and your Platform Merit rises automatically.

Because Platform Merit reflects current conduct rather than work quality, completing jobs, receiving 5-star reviews, verifying your identity, purchasing credits, or upgrading to a paid subscription does not increase Platform Merit. You cannot pay to raise your Platform Merit or remove active warnings.

## Platform Merit display bands

Your profile displays your current Platform Merit score along with a band label based on your remaining merit:

- **100 (Trusted):** Clean record with no active warnings and full platform access.
- **98 to 99 (Record):** Minor active warning noted on your private account record, with normal site access.
- **93 to 97 (Limited):** Minor automatic limits apply, such as not being able to post urgent jobs and having new marketplace asset listings routed through review.
- **85 to 92 (Restricted):** Broader feature limits apply across new listings, forum posting, team invites, and daily proposal volume while active warnings remain.
- **84 and below (Frozen):** New proposals, new direct messages, and cashouts are paused until merit recovers above 84, though ongoing contracts and gig orders remain accessible.

The exact thresholds and warning durations are maintained by Ensemble and may be updated over time.

## Where to check your Platform Merit

Anyone viewing your profile on `/profile` (or your public profile link) can see your Platform Merit ring. Other users cannot see your warning text, private details, or specific merit deductions.

Only you can view your full Account Record, which lists your current Platform Merit, any active or past warnings, how much merit each warning deducts, when each warning expires, and your current account standing. You can view your Account Record from your profile performance section on `/profile`.

## Teams and star ratings

Team profiles do not have a separate Platform Merit ring. Whenever someone performs a team action (such as posting a team job or submitting a team proposal), Ensemble checks the Platform Merit and account standing of the individual member taking the action.

Star ratings remain completely separate from Platform Merit. Warnings and Platform Merit changes do not alter your star ratings, and high star ratings do not exempt an account from Platform Merit limits.
