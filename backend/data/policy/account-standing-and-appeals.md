# Account Standing and Appeals

Routes: `/account/appeal`, `/support/ticket`, `/profile`, `/settings`

In addition to automatic Platform Merit limits from warnings, Ensemble can apply an account standing restriction when rules are broken or an account needs protection.

## Automatic Platform Merit limits

Platform Merit is 100 minus your active warning points. Nothing automatic happens while it stays above 60. When new warnings take it down to certain levels, a short automatic limit starts and runs for a set time, even if your merit recovers sooner: Limited for 1 day at 60, a Mute for 1 day at 55 and 2 days at 50, Restricted for 2 days at 45, Frozen access for 3 days at 40, and a suspension at 30 (3 days) or 20 (7 days). A Mute pauses forum posts, marketplace comments and new conversations; existing job, gig and contract threads stay open. What is running and when it ends shows under your merit on `/profile`, in your Account Record, and on the standing bar ("How to fix"). Staff can end a limit early, and each one can be appealed. When a limit ends you get a notification; when Frozen access ends it says "You're unfrozen".

## Types of account standing

- **Active:** Normal account status with full access (subject only to any active Platform Merit limits).
- **Forum Timeout:** Temporary restriction on forum writing. You can still read forums and use all other parts of Ensemble (jobs, gigs, marketplace, inbox, editor, contracts, and payouts).
- **Suspended:** Read-only access across Ensemble for a set period (such as 1 to 30 days manually, or 3 to 7 days automatically when Platform Merit drops to 30 or 20). You cannot post listings, send proposals, message in chat, submit deliverables, buy credits, or cash out while suspended.
- **Locked:** Applied during an investigation (for example, suspected account compromise, impersonation, or fraudulent identity documents). Access is restricted to appeals, tickets, and active disputes until an investigation concludes.
- **Banned:** Temporary or permanent removal of normal platform access for severe violations or confirmed dispute convictions. Banned member accounts can sign in only to submit an appeal and view their tickets and disputes.

If you have an active contract or gig order when an account is suspended or banned, the other party can open a ticket or dispute so Ensemble can resolve escrow fairly. Account restrictions never automatically seize wallet balances.

## What others see vs. your private Account Record

Other users on `/profile` and in marketplace listings only see short standing labels (such as Timeout, Suspended, Locked, or Banned) and your Platform Merit ring. If an account is banned, the general ban reason (and a link to any related public dispute) may also appear on the profile.

Your private Account Record on `/profile` and `/account/appeal` shows your full standing details, warning descriptions, expiration dates, timeouts, and recent standing history. Other members never see your individual warning notes or private record.

## How to appeal a restriction or warning

Each penalty on your account has its own appeal. A ban, a lock, a suspension, each forum timeout, each warning, each automatic punishment, and each credit hold is appealed separately, and a decision on one never changes the others.

1. Open `/account/appeal` (banned and locked members are taken there when they sign in; everyone else can open it from the standing bar or **Cases**). It lists every active penalty.
2. Pick the penalty and choose **Appeal this**. Explain what happened in your own words and add any evidence.
3. Staff reply on that appeal's ticket in **Cases**. You can have an open appeal for each penalty at the same time.

The **Appeal an account penalty** option on `/support/ticket` sends you to `/account/appeal`, where you pick the penalty. The area-specific appeal options for Forum, Marketplace, or Jobs and Gigs are for removed posts, listings, or jobs, not account penalties.

## Appeal outcomes and recovery

- **Appeal accepted:** Only the appealed penalty changes. A ban, lock, suspension, forum timeout, automatic punishment, or credit hold usually lifts within 24 hours; staff may lift it right away or shorten it to an earlier end date instead. An accepted warning appeal rescinds that warning at once, so its points stop counting against your Platform Merit.
- **Warning rescinded or pardoned:** If a warning is rescinded after review, its merit deduction ends immediately. A full pardon clears all active warning deductions and restores Active standing.
- **Appeal denied:** That penalty stays in place, and it can be appealed again after 12 months (or never, if it was denied permanently). Your other penalties can still be appealed. If an appeal was abusive, staff may also suspend or ban the account when they deny it. You cannot bypass any restriction, warning, or ban by purchasing credits or upgrading to Premium or Business.
- **The decision** is posted on the appeal ticket as a decision card: which penalty, what changes, any note from staff, and when it can be appealed again.

## Deactivating your account is not a punishment

Voluntarily deactivating your account in `/settings` is not a punishment and does not reduce Platform Merit. While deactivated, your profile is hidden from other members and reactivation stays locked for a 72-hour cooldown before you can turn the account back on.
