# Account Standing and Appeals

Routes: `/account/appeal`, `/support/ticket`, `/profile`, `/settings`

In addition to automatic Platform Merit limits from warnings, Ensemble can apply an account standing restriction when rules are broken or an account needs protection.

## Types of account standing

- **Active:** Normal account status with full access (subject only to any active Platform Merit limits).
- **Forum Timeout:** Temporary restriction on forum writing. You can still read forums and use all other parts of Ensemble (jobs, gigs, marketplace, inbox, editor, contracts, and payouts).
- **Suspended:** Read-only access across Ensemble for a set period (such as 1 to 30 days manually, or 7 to 14 days automatically after high warning totals). You cannot post listings, send proposals, message in chat, submit deliverables, buy credits, or cash out while suspended.
- **Locked:** Applied during an investigation (for example, suspected account compromise, impersonation, or fraudulent identity documents). Access is restricted to appeals, tickets, and active disputes until an investigation concludes.
- **Banned:** Temporary or permanent removal of normal platform access for severe violations or confirmed dispute convictions. Banned member accounts can sign in only to submit an appeal and view their tickets and disputes.

If you have an active contract or gig order when an account is suspended or banned, the other party can open a ticket or dispute so Ensemble can resolve escrow fairly. Account restrictions never automatically seize wallet balances.

## What others see vs. your private Account Record

Other users on `/profile` and in marketplace listings only see short standing labels (such as Timeout, Suspended, Locked, or Banned) and your Platform Merit ring. If an account is banned, the general ban reason (and a link to any related public dispute) may also appear on the profile.

Your private Account Record on `/profile` and `/account/appeal` shows your full standing details, warning descriptions, expiration dates, timeouts, and recent standing history. Other members never see your individual warning notes or private record.

## How to appeal a restriction or warning

If your account is restricted, suspended, locked, or banned, you can appeal in two ways:

1. **Banned or Locked accounts:** Sign in and you will be routed to `/account/appeal`, where you can review your Account Record, submit an appeal directly, reply to your open appeal tickets, and view or reply to existing disputes.
2. **Active, Timed-out, or Suspended accounts:** Go to `/support/ticket` and select **Safety and appeals** then **Appeal an account penalty** (or the area-specific appeal option for Forum, Marketplace, or Jobs and Gigs).

Explain clearly which restriction or warning you are appealing and what happened in your own words.

## Appeal outcomes and recovery

- **Appeal accepted:** If an appeal for a ban, lock, or suspension is accepted, the standing restriction lifts within 24 hours. Any unexpired warnings still on the account continue to determine your Platform Merit band until they expire.
- **Warning rescinded or pardoned:** If a warning is rescinded after review, its merit deduction ends immediately. A full pardon clears all active warning deductions and restores Active standing.
- **Appeal denied:** The standing remains in place. You cannot bypass any restriction, warning, or ban by purchasing credits or upgrading to Premium or Business.

## Deactivating your account is not a punishment

Voluntarily deactivating your account in `/settings` is not a punishment and does not reduce Platform Merit. While deactivated, your profile is hidden from other members and reactivation stays locked for a 72-hour cooldown before you can turn the account back on.
