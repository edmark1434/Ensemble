# Ensemble Badges

Routes: `/notifications`, `/profile`

Badges recognize milestones and achievements you have earned across Ensemble. Badges are separate from star ratings and Platform Merit: earning or losing a badge never changes your Platform Merit, and warnings do not delete your earned badges.

## Badge catalog

Ensemble currently awards milestone badges across four main areas:

- **Early Access and Profile Setup:**
  - **Alpha Tester:** Awarded for signing up during Ensemble's alpha period.
  - **Beta Tester:** Awarded for participating in beta access or via platform beta grant.
  - **Profile Complete:** Awarded when your profile checklist reaches 100% (avatar, tagline, bio, introduction, skills, and portfolio item).
- **Freelancer Ranks:** Earned by completing successful jobs or gig orders as a freelancer - **Fresh Freelancer** (1 close), **Rising Freelancer** (10 closes), **Elite Freelancer** (50 closes), and **Grand Freelancer** (100 closes).
- **Client Ranks:** Earned by completing successful funded jobs or gig orders as a client - **Fresh Client** (1 close), **Rising Client** (10 closes), **Elite Client** (50 closes), and **Grand Client** (100 closes).
- **Creator Ranks:** Earned by publishing assets on the Asset Marketplace - **Fresh Creator** (1 published asset), **Rising Creator** (10 published assets), **Elite Creator** (50 published assets), and **Grand Creator** (100 published assets).

Only genuinely completed contracts or gig orders count toward Freelancer and Client ranks; cancelled, expired-without-hire, or refunded-without-delivery work does not count. Likewise, only published marketplace assets count toward Creator ranks.

## How to claim and pin badges

When you qualify for a badge, Ensemble sends a pending badge notification to `/notifications`. Badges are not shown automatically until you claim them:

1. Open `/notifications`.
2. Find the badge notification and click **Claim**.
3. Go to `/profile` and open the badge editor in your profile sidebar to pin and reorder up to **5 badges** on your public profile.

You can claim pending badges even if your Platform Merit is in the Frozen band, though claiming is disabled while an account is suspended, locked, or banned.

## Visibility and rules

- **Public visibility:** Up to 5 pinned badges appear on your public profile and creator cards. If your account is Banned, Locked, or in the Frozen merit band, your pinned badges are hidden from public view until your standing or merit recovers, though they remain saved on your account.
- **No paid shortcuts:** Paid plans (Premium or Business) and credit purchases do not unlock milestone badges. Identity verification and forum, chat, or video editor activity also do not grant badges.
- **Teams:** Badges belong to individual user accounts rather than team profiles. Work completed on a team counts toward the acting user's personal badge progress.
- **Revocation:** Badges are only revoked if they were granted by mistake or obtained through fraud.
