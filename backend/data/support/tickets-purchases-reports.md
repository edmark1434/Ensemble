# Support Tickets, Purchase Issues, and Ticket Categories

Route: `/support/ticket`

## Signing in and submitting a ticket

You must be signed in to submit a ticket on `/support/ticket`. When you submit, provide a clear subject, choose the issue area and specific ticket type, fill in any requested context fields (such as selecting the related project, order, listing, contract, or job), write a clear description, and optionally attach up to 4 screenshots or files (maximum 8 MB per file).

Once submitted, you receive a ticket number. If your account is banned or locked, you can submit an appeal and reply to existing tickets and disputes directly on `/account/appeal`.

## Available ticket categories

The ticket form groups issues by area so your request reaches the right specialist team:

- **Support:**
  - *Account:* Sign-in and password, Account compromised, Identity verification, Business verification (for a team), Profile and settings, Teams, Delete account or export data, Account bug report.
  - *Billing:* Subscriptions and plans, Credits and balance, Payouts and withdrawals, Charges and receipts, Team funds and distributions, Billing bug report.
  - *Video Editing Platform:* Editor problems (crashes, freezes, slowness), Export and rendering, Uploads and storage, Project files, Sharing and collaboration, Video editor bug report.
  - *Messaging:* Inbox, chat, and calls; Notifications and email; Messaging bug report.
  - *Safety and appeals:* Report a user, Copyright claim, Appeal an account penalty (this one opens `/account/appeal`, where you pick which penalty to appeal; each penalty has its own appeal), Safety tools bug report.
  - *General:* Website bug report, Other.
- **Forum:** Posts and replies, Forum groups, Report a post or member, Appeal a forum action, Forum bug report.
- **Marketplace:** Seller verification, Listing review, Asset purchase and download, File is wrong or broken, Marketplace refunds, Report a listing or seller, Asset reviews, Appeal a marketplace action, Marketplace bug report.
- **Jobs and Gigs:** Job and gig listings, Proposals and hiring, Contracts, milestones, and orders, Workspaces and team projects, Delivery and disputes, Cancellations and refunds, Report a listing or user, Gig reviews, Appeal a jobs action, Jobs and gigs bug report.

## Reporting a bug

Every category has its own bug report type, so the team that looks after that part of Ensemble gets it. Pick the category where the bug happened, then its bug report type (for example **Marketplace -> Marketplace bug report**). If it is not in any listed area, use **General -> Website bug report**. The form asks where it happened, a link to the page, the steps to make it happen, what you expected and what happened instead, how often it happens, and whether you can still finish what you were doing. Bugs that stop you from finishing are handled first. A screenshot or screen recording helps. Your browser and the page you came from are saved with the ticket automatically.

## Ticket statuses

Tickets move through three main stages: **Open**, **In Progress**, and **Closed**. Always keep sensitive passwords, full card numbers, or private authentication codes out of ticket attachments and messages.

## Purchase, credit, and contract problems

- **Credit top-ups or receipts:** Choose **Billing -> Credits and balance** or **Charges and receipts** and include the payment time and receipt ID so support can match the transaction.
- **Asset purchase or broken file:** Choose **Marketplace -> Asset purchase and download**, **File is wrong or broken**, or **Marketplace refunds**, and name the asset and purchase time.
- **Contract or milestone disagreement:** Use the contract's dispute workflow on `/contracts/dispute-form` for active contract escrow disputes, or submit a **Jobs and Gigs** ticket for listing, proposal, or order issues. A **Delivery and disputes** ticket gets help around a dispute, but only a dispute filed from the contract lets staff rule on it and hold the escrow. For full details on reporting and disputes, see the Reports and Disputes guide.
