# Projects, Editor, Teams, Workspaces, and Dashboard

Routes: `/projects`, `/projects/personal`, `/projects/shared`, `/projects/team`, `/projects/trash`, `/projects/select`, `/teams`, `/workspaces`, `/dashboard`

## Video projects and browser editor

- **Projects List (`/projects`):** View and organize video editing projects across **Personal** (`/projects/personal`), **Shared** (`/projects/shared`), **Team** (`/projects/team`), and **Trash** (`/projects/trash`) tabs, in grid or compact view.
- **Create or Select Project (`/projects/select`):** Choose a project format or configuration to start a new edit.
- **Open in Editor:** Opening a project launches the collaborative browser video editor where team members with edit access can work on the timeline together, use AI Auto Captioning and Auto Dead-Air Cleanup (based on plan), and export deliverables.

Team and project limits depend on your current subscription plan (Free, Premium, or Business) and your account standing. Personal projects remain usable even under Restricted or Frozen Platform Merit bands, though starting new shared or team projects is blocked if your merit is Restricted or lower.

## Teams and invitations

Route: `/teams`

Users can create teams (subject to subscription plan limits and account standing), set visibility (Public or Private) and join policy (Open or Approval), manage team member roles, and invite collaborators with a team invite link or join code. Open a specific team from `/teams` to view its members, jobs/gigs, tasks, and business verification option.

## Contract workspaces

Route: `/workspaces`

Workspaces organize active **Job Workspaces** (created from hired job proposals) and **Gig Workspaces** (created from accepted and funded gig orders). Inside a workspace, team members and clients can collaborate on tasks, link video editor projects, and track progress through four stages:

1. **Waiting:** Awaiting initial contract funding into escrow.
2. **Active:** Escrow funded; contributors update the taskboard and submit milestone deliverables.
3. **Rate and Review:** All milestones approved; a 24-hour mutual double-blind rating window opens while the taskboard locks read-only.
4. **Archived:** Reviews completed or rating window closed; the workspace becomes a read-only archive.

## Delivery dashboard

Route: `/dashboard`

The delivery dashboard provides tabs for tracking ongoing deliverables across `/dashboard/tasks`, `/dashboard/review`, `/dashboard/rate`, `/dashboard/archived`, and `/dashboard/flow` (the Delivery Workflow guide).
