# Personal Identity Verification (KYC)

Route: `/account-verification-status`

Personal Identity Verification (KYC) confirms your identity before you fund escrow, offer or accept contracts, publish marketplace services or paid listings, or withdraw wallet earnings.

## How personal verification works

Open `/account-verification-status` to check your current status or begin verification. The workflow covers four steps:

1. **Valid ID Submission:** Upload a clear photo of an accepted government ID such as a Passport, Driver's License, or National ID.
2. **Data Extraction and Form:** Your ID details populate the verification form for review.
3. **Biometric Liveness Check:** Complete a brief camera check to confirm real-time physical ownership of the identity.
4. **Review and Approval:** Submitted details are reviewed (typically 1 to 3 business days).

If you begin on desktop and prefer your phone camera for the photo and liveness check, you can switch to your mobile device using the verification session.

## Verification statuses and validity

On `/account-verification-status`, you may see:

- **Not started:** No verification has been completed yet. Select the start option to begin.
- **Pending review / In progress:** Your submission is currently being checked.
- **Identity Verified:** Your identity is confirmed and verified features are unlocked. Approved personal verification shows its validity expiration date (valid for one year from acceptance).
- **Expired or Abandoned:** If a session timed out before completion or an approved verification expired, start a new session from `/account-verification-status`.

## Troubleshooting verification

- Make sure your ID photo is well-lit, uncropped, and free of glare, and that your account name and details match your ID.
- Submitting fraudulent identity documents is a serious violation that results in a severe Platform Merit penalty and an account lock or ban.
- If your verification appears stuck, was rejected unexpectedly, or needs manual review, submit a ticket on `/support/ticket` under **Support -> Account -> Identity verification**. Never attach full private ID photos to ordinary chat messages.
