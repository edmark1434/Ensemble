# Chat, Inbox, Calls, and Meetings

Route: `/inbox`

The Ensemble Inbox keeps client, freelancer, team, and marketplace communication organized in one place.

## Conversations and context

- **Direct and Group Chats:** Message other members directly or create group conversations. Authorized group members can manage group roles, membership, and group profile images.
- **Marketplace and Work Threads:** Conversations started from a job post, proposal, gig order, contract, or asset listing carry context linking back to the related item.

## Messaging features

Within conversations, supported features include:

- Sending text messages and file/media attachments
- Replying to specific messages
- Editing or deleting your own messages
- Adding message reactions and pinning important messages
- Seeing typing indicators, online status, and unread message counts

Note that if an account's Platform Merit drops into the **Frozen** band (84 or below), starting *new* direct messages or *new* inbox groups is paused, though you can still reply in existing job, gig, or contract threads. Suspended, locked, or banned accounts cannot send chat messages.

## Voice, video calls, and Google Meet

- **Browser Calls:** Ensemble supports direct and group audio/video calls directly in the browser (showing ringing, active, busy, rejected, or ended states). Camera and microphone access require browser permissions.
- **Google Meet:** Where available and connected to your Google account, you can schedule or launch Google Meet sessions from conversation threads and track requested, scheduled, active, or ended states.

For safety, never share passwords, verification codes, or private payment details in chat. If you receive spam, harassment, or scam messages in `/inbox`, report the user or file a ticket on `/support/ticket`.
