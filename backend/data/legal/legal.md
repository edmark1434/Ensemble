# Legal Information

Routes: `/landing/TermsOfService`, `/landing/PrivacyPolicy`, `/terms-of-services`

Ensemble's Terms of Service and Privacy Policy explain the rules for using the platform and how personal data and project media are handled. For full wording, always read the current rendered legal pages directly. The support assistant can summarize these pages but does not provide legal advice.

## Terms of Service summary

Route: `/landing/TermsOfService`

The Terms of Service cover 10 key areas:

1. **Acceptance of Terms:** Using Ensemble means agreeing to the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.
2. **Description of Service:** Ensemble provides a browser-based collaborative video editor, AI tools, job and gig marketplaces, asset marketplace, and escrow payment workflows.
3. **User Accounts and Identity Verification (KYC):** Users must be at least 13 years old (and meet legal age requirements for KYC financial transactions), provide accurate information, protect account credentials, and not transfer accounts.
4. **User Content:** You retain ownership of your original content, project media, and assets, while granting Ensemble the limited license needed to host, process, display, and deliver them for platform features.
5. **Prohibited Conduct:** Violating laws or third-party intellectual property, distributing malware, bypassing escrow or verification/security checks, and disrupting workspaces are prohibited.
6. **Payment, Escrow, and Milestone Policy:** Credits have no direct cash value outside official cashout procedures. Milestone credits are held in escrow until approved or auto-approved (5 calendar days for Job Contracts and 3 days for Gig Contracts if no review, revision, or dispute is submitted). Overdue milestones may be extended or cancelled by the client for a 100% refund of that milestone. Either party may request Mutual Contract Cancellation, which auto-approves if unanswered for 72 hours, refunding unfinished escrow milestones to the client while keeping approved milestone payouts with the freelancer.
7. **Termination:** Ensemble may restrict, suspend, lock, or terminate accounts under its Platform Merit and moderation policies.
8. **Disclaimer of Warranties:** The service is provided on an "as is" and "as available" basis.
9. **Limitation of Liability:** Standard limits apply to indirect or consequential damages.
10. **Changes to Terms:** Continued use after updated terms take effect indicates acceptance.

## Privacy Policy summary

Route: `/landing/PrivacyPolicy`

The Privacy Policy covers 8 key areas:

1. **Information We Collect:** Account and KYC details (name, email, avatar, verification status), project and marketplace content (timelines, contracts, assets, comments), and technical/session logs.
2. **How We Use Information:** Powering collaborative editing, AI Auto Captioning, Auto Dead-Air Cleanup, escrow and wallet transactions, Asset Library delivery, and security alerts. Private footage is not used to train public AI models.
3. **Information Sharing:** Shared only with explicit project/contract counterparties, trusted infrastructure/payment processors under confidentiality, or when required by law.
4. **Data Security:** Encryption in transit and at rest, isolated escrow accounting, and access controls.
5. **Your Privacy Rights:** Rights to access, correct, or request deletion of personal data, and opt out of non-essential promotional messages.
6. **Cookies and Tracking:** Essential cookies and local storage keep you signed in and preserve theme and workspace state.
7. **Data Retention:** Data is kept as needed for service operation and legal/escrow compliance; finalized account deletion requests purge personal data within 30 days.
8. **Contact:** Privacy questions can be sent to the Data Protection Officer listed on `/landing/PrivacyPolicy` (`privacy@ensemble.app`).

## Custom participant terms

Route: `/terms-of-services`

Signed-in users can create and manage reusable Terms of Service templates for Jobs and Gigs on `/terms-of-services` (for example, specifying revision rules, delivery terms, or portfolio rights). Custom contract or gig terms are separate from Ensemble's platform legal policies and should be reviewed carefully before sending or accepting an offer.
