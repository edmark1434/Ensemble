# Job Market and Job Postings

Routes: `/jobs/postings`, `/jobs/applied`, `/jobs/saved-posts`, `/jobs/my-job-post`, `/jobs/create`, `/jobs/create/team`, `/jobs/info`

The Job Market connects clients who need custom video editing, motion graphics, sound design, or creative post-production with individual freelancers and creative teams.

## Browsing and managing job posts

- **Job Postings (`/jobs/postings`):** Browse open job listings, filter by skills or criteria, view details, and save posts for later.
- **Saved Posts (`/jobs/saved-posts`):** Revisit bookmarked job listings.
- **Applied Jobs (`/jobs/applied`):** Track jobs you have applied to.
- **My Job Posts (`/jobs/my-job-post`):** Clients manage their published job listings, review incoming proposals, extend application windows, or close listings.
- **Job Ranking Guide (`/jobs/info`):** Read an in-app breakdown of how job visibility and ranking work.

## Posting a job (individual or team)

Route: `/jobs/create` (or `/jobs/create/team` for team job posts)

Verified clients in good standing can publish a job post by choosing a title, plain-text deliverables scope, budget (open bid or ranged credits), turnaround estimate, skill tags, custom thumbnail or reference brief attachments, optional screening questions or milestones, and an intake application window (`12h`, `24h`, `3d`, `7d`, or `14d`).

- **Application windows and countdown:** Every job post runs on a timed intake window so applicants know when decisions will be made.
- **Urgent jobs:** Urgent job flags are restricted if your Platform Merit is in the Limited band (93 to 97) or lower.
- **Refund protections:** If an open job finishes its application window with zero applicants (or is closed early with zero proposals), the posting fee is refunded to the client's wallet. Likewise, if a job closes or expires without hiring anyone, applicants' proposal submission fees are refunded automatically. Check the Current Ensemble Platform Settings document for current posting and platform fee details.

## How job ranking works

Job postings are scored on a 100-point scale (plus optional subscription visibility and freelancer skill-match bonuses) across three areas:

1. **Job Post Content (up to 25 points, editable anytime):** Clear title and 150+ character deliverables scope (7.5 pts), realistic budget or focused range (6.25 pts), custom thumbnail or reference attachments (5 pts), 3 or more specific skill tags (3.75 pts plus up to 2 pts skill-match bonus), and timeline, workflow terms, or screening setup (2.5 pts).
2. **Client Account Standing (up to 35 points plus subscription boost):** Weighted star rating with a fair 4.2-star starting baseline for new clients (22.75 pts), Client Platform Merit score (7.70 pts), historical hire rate starting at a 100% baseline for first-time posters (4.55 pts), and subscription visibility boost (Business +9 pts, Premium +3 pts, Free 0 pts).
3. **Live Market Signals (up to 40 points):** Intake window freshness (19.5 pts, with a rebound near the end if few proposals arrived), low proposal congestion per open seat (15 pts), community bookmarks from freelancers (3.75 pts), and an automatic urgency spotlight when under 24 hours remain with low proposal volume (1.75 pts).

Feed refreshes use score-weighted rotation so well-crafted new posts and underserved listings stay discoverable alongside established clients.

## The Rising badge on job posts

New job posts earn the green **Rising** badge when they are published within the last 7 days, have fewer than 3 proposals per open seat, belong to a first-time client with no prior reviews and Platform Merit of 95 or higher, and achieve a high brief quality score.
