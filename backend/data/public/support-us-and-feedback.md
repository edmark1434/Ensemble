# Feedback and Supporting Ensemble

Routes: `/landing/SendAFeedback`, `/landing/SupportUs`

## Sending product feedback

Route: `/landing/SendAFeedback`

Signed-in Ensemble members can share product feedback on `/landing/SendAFeedback` by choosing a category (**General Insight**, **Feature Request**, **Bug Report**, or **UI/UX Suggestion**), selecting a star rating, and writing comments.

Feedback is intended for product suggestions, feature ideas, and general UI/UX impressions. It is not a customer support ticket and does not create a ticket number or direct support reply thread. If you need help with an account, payment, contract, bug blocking your work, or restriction, submit a ticket on `/support/ticket` instead.

## Supporting the project

Route: `/landing/SupportUs`

The Support Us page highlights non-financial ways community members can support Ensemble, including starring the RavenLabs Development project repository and sharing the platform with fellow video editors, creators, and remote post-production teams.
