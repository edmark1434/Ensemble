# Ensemble Use Cases by User Type

Routes: `/landing/usecase/client`, `/landing/usecase/freelancer`, `/landing/usecase/asset-creator`, `/landing/usecase/enthusiast`

Ensemble brings Clients, Freelancers, Asset Creators, and Enthusiasts into one unified account. You can use the role-specific use case guides under `/landing/usecase/client` to explore each workflow.

## Clients (`/landing/usecase/client`)

Clients hire solo editors or multi-member creative teams with escrow protection:

- **Two ways to hire:** Post a job on `/jobs/create` and approve an individual or team proposal, or order a packaged service on `/discovery` or `/gigs/services` and offer a contract once accepted.
- **Protected milestones:** Fund milestone budgets in escrow so credits are only released after you review and approve deliverables.
- **Live tracking and communication:** Monitor progress on `/dashboard`, chat or hold video calls in `/inbox`, and lock clear deliverables and revision terms upfront.
- **Prerequisite:** Complete one-time Identity Verification (KYC) on `/account-verification-status` before funding escrow or signing contracts.

## Freelancers (`/landing/usecase/freelancer`)

Freelancers land steady creative work with guaranteed escrow payouts:

- **Find work two ways:** Apply to open jobs on `/jobs/postings` with solo or team proposals, or publish 3-tier service packages on `/gigs/create` so clients can order directly.
- **Edit in the browser:** Work directly on shared timelines, use AI Auto Captioning and Auto Dead-Air Cleanup, and submit milestone deliveries through `/dashboard`.
- **Protected payouts:** Escrow holds milestone funds before you start, and approved payouts credit your wallet for cashout via `/settings`.

## Asset Creators (`/landing/usecase/asset-creator`)

Asset Creators monetize reusable production media on `/assets`:

- **Supported formats:** Sell Template Videos, multi-asset Bundles, Images, Videos, and Audios with browser previews and tags.
- **Instant library delivery:** Buyers receive immediate access in their Purchased library and can import assets directly into the collaborative video editor.
- **Passive credit earnings:** Track purchases and withdraw wallet earnings when eligible.

## Creators and Enthusiasts (`/landing/usecase/enthusiast`)

Creators and Enthusiasts use Ensemble's studio for personal or collaborative video projects:

- **Browser studio:** Create projects on `/projects/select` and edit multi-track timelines directly in the browser with real-time team sync.
- **AI productivity tools:** Speed up rough cuts with one-click Auto Dead-Air Cleanup and searchable AI Auto Captioning.
- **Seamless upgrades:** Start creating right away and complete KYC whenever you are ready to hire talent, apply for paid jobs, or sell services and assets.
