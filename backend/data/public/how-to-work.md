# How to Work and Earn on Ensemble

Route: `/landing/HowToWork`

Verified Ensemble members can earn credits in three main ways: offering packaged Services (Gigs), submitting proposals on Job Posts, or selling digital media in the Asset Marketplace.

## Way 1: Sell packaged Services (Gigs)

1. **Publish a Service:** Create a service listing on `/gigs/create` with Basic, Standard, and Premium packages, pricing, turnaround times, work samples, and client requirements.
2. **Receive Service Orders:** Clients discover your service on `/gigs/services` or `/discovery` and submit service orders with their brief.
3. **Review and Accept:** Inspect the order in `/gigs/orders` and accept orders that fit your schedule and slot capacity.
4. **Receive the Contract Offer:** Once you accept, the Client offers an escrow-backed contract.
5. **Deliver Milestones and Earn:** Submit deliverables through `/dashboard` and receive protected escrow payouts into your wallet as milestones are approved.

## Way 2: Apply to Job Posts

1. **Browse Open Jobs:** Find matching individual or team production briefs on `/jobs/postings`.
2. **Submit a Proposal:** Send a proposal with your pitch, rate, and milestone plan. Track sent pitches on `/jobs/proposals/sent`.
3. **Get Shortlisted and Approved:** Discuss deliverables with the Client via `/inbox` and get approved.
4. **Sign the Contract Offer:** Review and accept the offered contract before starting work.
5. **Complete Milestones and Withdraw:** Deliver each milestone on `/dashboard` and withdraw eligible wallet earnings from `/settings`.

## Way 3: Sell on the Asset Marketplace

1. **Upload Media:** Open `/assets` and create a listing for Template Videos, multi-asset Bundles, Images, Videos, or Audios.
2. **Set Pricing and Licensing:** Provide a title, description, tags, credit price, and preview thumbnail.
3. **Publish to the Marketplace:** Publish your listing (listings may enter review if required by your account standing or platform rules).
4. **Earn Credits on Purchases:** Buyers receive access to the protected file in their Purchased library while you keep ownership of your listing and earn credit proceeds in your wallet.
