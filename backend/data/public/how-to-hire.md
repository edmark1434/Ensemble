# How to Hire on Ensemble

Route: `/landing/HowToHire`

Every Ensemble account can act as both a Client and a Freelancer. Before funding escrow or signing contracts, complete your one-time Identity Verification (KYC) on `/account-verification-status`.

## Method 1: Hire through job posts

1. **Post a Job:** Create a job post on `/jobs/create` (or `/jobs/create/team` for a team) detailing the editing style, deliverables, timeline, and budget. Both individual Freelancers and multi-member Teams can apply.
2. **Receive Proposals:** Review tailored solo or team proposals with portfolio reels, proposed milestone breakdowns, and turnaround commitments on `/jobs/my-job-post`.
3. **Shortlist and Approve:** Compare candidates, message or hold a video call via `/inbox`, and shortlist and approve the individual or team proposal that fits best.
4. **Offer a Contract:** Send an official platform contract offer locking in scope, milestone deliverables, deadlines, and escrow payout amounts.
5. **Fund Escrow and Track Progress:** Deposit milestone credits into escrow, monitor progress on `/dashboard` and `/contracts`, and release funds as you approve each milestone.

## Method 2: Hire through Gigs and Services

1. **Find a Freelancer:** Explore `/discovery` or `/gigs/services` to compare Freelancer portfolios, service packages, pricing tiers, and turnaround times.
2. **Order a Service:** Select a service that matches your project and place a service order with your project brief, reference links, and target schedule.
3. **Freelancer Acceptance:** The Freelancer reviews your brief, aligns with you in `/inbox` if needed, and accepts the service order.
4. **Offer the Contract:** Once the Freelancer accepts your order, offer the official platform contract. Once signed and funded, the Freelancer is officially hired.
5. **Escrow and Delivery:** Milestone funds remain protected in escrow while you track completion on `/dashboard` and `/contracts` and release payout upon final approval.
