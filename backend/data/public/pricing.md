# Pricing and Membership Plans

Routes: `/landing/Pricing`, `/credits-subscriptions`

Ensemble offers subscription plans alongside credit packs. For exact live prices, billing periods, and trial days, refer to the Current Ensemble Plans document or visit `/credits-subscriptions`.

## Plan tiers and included capabilities

### Free

Designed for getting started and exploring the collaborative video studio:

- Maximum export quality: 720p (with watermark on exported files)
- Rendering speed and queue priority: Standard speed, low queue priority
- Editing tools: Basic editing tools
- Teams and projects: Up to 1 team, up to 3 members per team, and up to 3 collaborative projects
- Marketplace: Up to 1 asset post

### Premium

Designed for active freelancers and creators who need higher quality and AI tools:

- Maximum export quality: 1080p (no watermark on exported files)
- Rendering speed and queue priority: Accelerated speed, priority render queue
- Editing tools: Premium tools plus AI features (such as AI Auto Captioning and Auto Dead-Air Cleanup)
- Teams and projects: Up to 5 teams, up to 10 members per team, and up to 10 collaborative projects
- Marketplace and visibility: Up to 20 asset posts, displayed Premium badge, and +30 additional profile visibility in discovery and job/gig ranking

### Business

Designed for studios, agencies, and high-volume production teams:

- Maximum export quality: 2K-4K (no watermark on exported files)
- Rendering speed and queue priority: Maximum speed, top render queue priority
- Editing tools: Premium tools plus AI features
- Teams and projects: Unlimited teams, up to 20 members per team, and up to 20 collaborative projects
- Marketplace and visibility: Unlimited asset posts, displayed Business badge, and +90 additional profile visibility in discovery and job/gig ranking

## Managing or cancelling a subscription

You can subscribe, upgrade, downgrade, or view your active plan and trial status on `/credits-subscriptions` or in the Subscription tab of `/settings`. Paid plans never bypass Platform Merit limits or account standing penalties.
