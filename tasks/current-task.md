# Current Task: Readable ticket transcript

## Objective
The saved HTML transcript should read like the ticket conversation, with clear speakers, times, replies, people involved, and a summary at the end.

## Acceptance Criteria
1. User messages sit on the left and staff messages sit on the right.
2. System lines and internal notes are visually distinct.
3. Replies quote the earlier message, and links and line breaks stay readable.
4. People involved and a short summary remain at the end of the page.
5. Generating the transcript again replaces the older plain layout with this conversation page.
