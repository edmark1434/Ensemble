# Current Task: Unify Job Posting, Port Milestone Studio to Proposals & Route All Contracts to Project Workspaces
# Current Task: Remove Category and Location Display Under About Tab in Teams

## Objective
Implement the approved architectural redesign:
1. **Unified Job Posting**: Consolidate client job creation into a single 4-step wizard (Core Info, Budget & Skills, Terms & Workflow, Review & Post). Remove client-side milestone creation and eliminate confusing "Individual" vs "Team" job posting splits for clients. Clients no longer specify "Positions Needed" (fixed/default to 1 contract workspace).
2. **Port Milestone Studio to Proposals (`3_proposal_milestones.tsx`)**: Move the high-craft Milestone Studio builder into the Proposal flow (Step 3). Applicants (Solo Freelancers or Teams via `acting_team_id`) define the milestone phases, task checklists, duration hours, and budget percentages.
3. **Bidding Model Simplification & Clean Filters**: Support only Budget Range (capped at maximum budget ceiling) and Open Bid (with no ceiling limit). Remove "Project Type" filters, "Pooled" budget type filters, "Positions Needed" filters, and thumbnail badges from the marketplace.
4. **Applicant Identity & Representation**: Ask at the start of proposal creation whether applying as Myself (Individual Freelancer) or As a Team (selecting an existing user team), persisting `acting_team_id`.
5. **Spacious & Clean Review Experience**: Expand proposal creation width to `max-w-6xl 2xl:max-w-7xl` and upgrade Step 4 (`4_proposal_review.tsx`) with a neutral, crisp Milestone Breakdown Data Table.
6. **Backend & Workspace Routing**: Ensure all accepted contracts (both Solo and Team) automatically initialize a dedicated Project Workspace with taskboards generated from proposed milestones.
7. **Ranking Engine & Seed Dataset Alignment**: Update `job_ranking.ts`, `/jobs/info` guide, and seed files (`SeedJobsDataset.js`, `SeedJobs.js`, `job_datasets.tsx`) to purge legacy team pool entries, client milestones, and headcount splits. Re-seeded live database to 100% unified jobs.
Remove the category and location display under the "About" tab in Teams so that the About card focuses cleanly on the team description.
