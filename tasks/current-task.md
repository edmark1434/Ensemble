# Current Task: Schedule a ticket close in 24 hours

## Objective
Staff can schedule an open ticket to close 24 hours from now. The chat and the users on the ticket are notified when it is scheduled, and notified again when it closes.

## Acceptance Criteria
1. A staff member can confirm "Close in 24 hours" on an open ticket.
2. The ticket chat and the users involved receive a notice immediately.
3. About 24 hours later the ticket closes, a transcript is saved, and the users are notified again.
4. Staff can cancel the scheduled close before it happens. Cancelling is announced in the chat.
5. The existing 7-day inactivity close still runs when nobody scheduled a closer time.
