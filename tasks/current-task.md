# Current Task: Choose when a ticket closes

## Objective
Staff pick when an open ticket closes. The chat and the users on the ticket are notified when the time is set, and notified again when it closes.

## Acceptance Criteria
1. A staff member can choose 1 hour, 6 hours, 1 day, 3 days, 7 days, or a custom date and time.
2. The ticket chat and the users involved receive a notice immediately, using the chosen time.
3. At that time the ticket closes, a transcript is saved, and the users are notified again.
4. Staff can change or cancel the scheduled close before it happens. Cancelling is announced in the chat.
5. The existing 7-day inactivity close still runs when nobody scheduled a closer time.
